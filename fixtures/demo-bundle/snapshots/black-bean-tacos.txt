## Version 1: Black Bean Tacos
with Lime Crema

yields 2, 4
Smoky beans, crisp onion and a tangy lime crema, now with melty Monterey Jack.
meta {"content_type":"recipe","difficulty":1,"prep_time":"PT20M","tags":["vegetarian"]}

### Version 1 at 1 serving (derived)

- 3 tortillas Flour Tortillas [scaled]
- ½ can Black Beans [scaled]
- ¼ cup Onion [scaled]
- ¼ cup Monterey Jack Cheese [scaled]
- ½ lime Lime [scaled]
- 1 TBSP Sour Cream [scaled]
- 1½ tsp Taco Spice Blend [scaled]
- ½ tsp Olive Oil [scaled; pantry]

0. Finely chop the onion. Drain and rinse the beans. Halve the lime; in a small bowl, mix the sour cream with a squeeze of lime juice.
1. Heat ½ tsp olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook half the onion until soft, 3-4 minutes. Add the beans and taco spice; mash lightly and cook until warmed through. [scaled]
2. Warm the tortillas. Fill with the beans, then top with the cheese, the remaining onion and the lime crema.
