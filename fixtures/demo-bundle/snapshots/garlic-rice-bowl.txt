## Version 1: Garlic Rice Bowl
with Scallions and Soy

yields 2
Fragrant garlic rice topped with scallions and a drizzle of soy, for one or two.
meta {"content_type":"recipe","difficulty":1,"prep_time":"PT25M","tags":["vegetarian","quick"]}

### Version 1 at 1 serving (derived)

- ⅓ cup + 2 tsp Jasmine Rice [scaled]
- 1 clove Garlic [scaled]
- 1 stalk Scallions [scaled]
- 1½ tsp Soy Sauce [scaled]
- ½ tsp Sesame Oil [scaled]
- to taste Salt [pantry]

0. Mince the garlic. Thinly slice the scallions, keeping the whites and greens apart.
1. Heat the sesame oil in a small pot over medium heat. Add the garlic and scallion whites; cook 1 minute. Stir in the rice, ¾ cup water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer until tender, 15-18 minutes. [scaled]
2. Fluff the rice and divide between bowls. Drizzle with the soy sauce and top with the scallion greens.
