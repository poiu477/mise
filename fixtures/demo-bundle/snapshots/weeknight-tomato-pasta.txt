## Version 1: Weeknight Tomato Pasta
with Garlic and Parmesan

yields 2, 4
A pantry pasta for busy nights: a quick tomato sauce, plenty of garlic and a handful of cheese.
meta {"content_type":"recipe","difficulty":1,"prep_time":"PT25M","tags":["vegetarian","quick"]}

### Version 1 at 1 serving (derived)

- 3 oz Spaghetti [scaled]
- ½ can Crushed Tomatoes [scaled]
- 1 clove Garlic [scaled]
- ½ onion Yellow Onion [scaled]
- 2 TBSP Parmesan [scaled]
- 1 pinch Red Pepper Flakes [scaled]
- 1½ tsp Olive Oil [scaled; pantry]
- to taste Salt [pantry]

0. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Peel and finely chop the onion. Mince the garlic.
1. Heat 1½ tsp olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until soft, 5-6 minutes. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes; cook 1 minute more. [scaled]
2. Stir in the crushed tomatoes and a pinch of salt. Simmer until slightly thickened, 8-10 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti until al dente, 9-11 minutes. Reserve ¼ cup cooking water, then drain. [scaled]
4. Toss the spaghetti with the sauce, adding splashes of reserved water as needed. Serve topped with the Parmesan.
