## Version 1: Lemon Herb Chicken
with a Garlic Pan Sauce

yields 2, 4
Golden chicken with a bright, buttery pan sauce made in the same skillet.
meta {"content_type":"recipe","difficulty":1,"prep_time":"PT30M","tags":[]}

### Version 1 at 1 serving (derived)

- 6 oz Chicken Breasts [scaled]
- ½ lemon Lemon [scaled]
- ½ clove Garlic [scaled]
- ⅛ oz Parsley [scaled]
- ½ packet (1 tsp) Chicken Stock Concentrate [scaled]
- 1½ tsp Butter [scaled]
- 1½ tsp Olive Oil [scaled; pantry]
- to taste Salt [pantry]
- to taste Black Pepper [pantry]

0. Pat the chicken dry and season all over with salt and pepper. Zest and halve the lemon. Mince the garlic and chop the parsley.
1. Heat 1½ tsp olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken until browned and cooked through, 5-7 minutes per side. Set aside. [scaled]
2. Lower the heat to medium. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds. Stir in the stock concentrate and 3½ TBSP water; simmer until reduced by half, 2-3 minutes. [scaled]
3. Off the heat, stir in the butter, the lemon zest and a squeeze of lemon juice. Slice the chicken, spoon over the sauce and scatter with parsley.
