Spiced Lentil Soup

Sasha Bloom

Red lentils, warm spices and a squeeze of lime.

Soup, Indian 10 min 25 min Serves 4 to 6
240 calories
  • 1 cup red lentils
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 lime
  1. Soften the onion in a little oil, then stir in the curry powder.
  2. Add the lentils and water and simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Season and finish with lime juice.
