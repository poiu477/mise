Chickpea Flatbread

Recipe by Mina Haddad

Yield: Serves 2

  • 1 cup chickpea flour
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt

  1. Whisk the flour, water, salt and half the oil. Rest for 30 minutes.

  2. Heat the rest of the oil in a skillet, pour in the batter and bake at 450°F for 15 minutes.
