Garlic Butter Green Beans

Blanched beans finished in a garlicky butter.

By Pat Okafor

Prep: 10 minutes, Cook: 8 minutes, Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Boil the beans in salted water for 4 minutes, then drain.
  2. Melt the butter, add the garlic and cook until fragrant.
  3. Toss the beans in the garlic butter and season.
